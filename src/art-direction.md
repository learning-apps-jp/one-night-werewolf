# 背景画像

Built-in ImageGenで生成し、プロジェクト内の`night-village.png`に保存。

## 最終プロンプト

Use case: illustration-story. Create one panoramic background illustration for a Japanese pass-and-play werewolf tablet game. Moonlit secluded medieval village at the edge of a pine forest, a large luminous ivory full moon in upper right, warm amber windows in a few distant small cottages, deep ink navy and desaturated blue teal mist, delicate stars, subtle grain and hand-painted cinematic storybook texture. Landscape 1536x1024. Entire center and left should remain very dark and low detail to support readable UI overlays; scenery concentrated in right third and along bottom edge. Refined mysterious rather than horror, welcoming to families. No people, no wolves, no typography, no borders, no interface elements.
